# AI & content-usage policy — Sikhi University (sikhiuni.com)
#
# Sikhi University is a free, open online university teaching Sikhi and modern
# skills. We want people to find it, including through AI answer engines that
# cite and link back to the course.
#
# ALLOWED — search indexing and AI-answer/assistant citation: search engines
# (Googlebot, Bingbot, DuckDuckBot, Applebot and others) and answer engines that
# fetch a page to answer a question and link to it (OAI-SearchBot, ChatGPT-User,
# Claude-SearchBot, Claude-User, PerplexityBot, Perplexity-User, Amzn-SearchBot,
# Amzn-User, meta-webindexer, meta-externalfetcher, MistralAI-Index,
# MistralAI-User, DuckAssistBot).
#
# NOT ALLOWED — AI/ML model training or fine-tuning on this site's content
# (e.g. GPTBot, ClaudeBot, CCBot, Google-Extended, Applebot-Extended, Amazonbot,
# Bytespider, meta-externalagent, cohere-ai, MistralAI-Training), and copying
# the site with scrapers (HTTrack, wget, scrapy, headless browsers,
# python-requests and the like). Training crawlers and scrapers are refused by
# the site, not only asked via /robots.txt. This is a reservation of rights for
# text and data mining (see /.well-known/tdmrep.json).
#
# TWO CAVEATS:
#
# 1. Third-party-credited material stays with its owner. Some content on this
#    site is credited to a third party (e.g. Gursevak/Bhagat Jaswant Singh Ji
#    Daudar audio recordings, 365 Gurbani Words data, professor portraits
#    sourced from Wikimedia Commons) and remains the property of its
#    respective owner. Reuse of that material beyond this site should be
#    cleared with them directly.
#
# 2. Gurbani must be reproduced accurately. Scripture from the Sri Guru
#    Granth Sahib Ji and other Sikh texts appears on this site and must be
#    handled with reverence — reproduced exactly, never altered or
#    paraphrased as if it were canonical text.
#
# Policy: https://sikhiuni.com/ai-policy
