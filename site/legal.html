  • Accreditation. Sikhi University is not an accredited university or degree-granting institution. Its courses and program ladder (Certificate through Giani) are for personal study and self-development only and confer no accredited academic standing or professional qualification.
  • Gurbani translations & interpretations. Interpretations and translations presented on the Platform are not the work or view of any single individual. They draw on established, named sources used across the Platform: the BaniDB English translation (api.banidb.com) shown in the course verse viewer; the classic commentaries the Platform teaches from — Faridkot Wala Teeka (ਫਰੀਦਕੋਟ ਵਾਲਾ ਟੀਕਾ) and Guru Granth Darpan (ਗੁਰੂ ਗ੍ਰੰਥ ਦਰਪਣ, Prof. Sahib Singh); the published works of the scholars each course is credited to (for example Prof. Sahib Singh, Bhai Vir Singh, Kavi Santokh Singh, and Giani Gian Singh); and Santhya audio recordings of Bhindran Taksal. As above, Gurbani is presented with reverence and to the best of our ability for accuracy; the Platform is not a religious authority, and we encourage you to verify it against authentic, authoritative sources.
  • Certifications. Certificates of completion are issued by Sikhi University itself, automatically upon passing a course's test, and each carries a verification ID checkable at /verify. They are not accredited degrees or professional credentials, and are not issued, endorsed, or recognised by any accredited institution, religious authority, or third party.

Privacy Policy

We practise data minimisation: we collect only what is needed to run the Platform, and we never sell personal data.

  • What we collect. If you create an account: your email address and your learning data (course progress, quiz results, certificates, and a display name you choose). If you contact us: the details you submit. Basic, privacy-respecting analytics may be collected in aggregate.
  • Security and activity logs. To protect accounts and investigate misuse, we keep an audit log of security-relevant actions (for example: sign-ins and failed sign-in attempts, two-factor changes, password changes, role and grade changes, data exports, and access that was refused). Each entry can include the IP address, approximate country and browser/device description of the request, plus a one-way hash of the session (never the session itself). IP address, country and device details are erased after 90 days. The rest of each entry is kept for up to 2 years (routine learning events, such as completed lessons, for up to 180 days), then deleted. You can see your own recent sign-ins and security changes, with the IP partly masked, on your profile page, and use "Sign out everywhere" there. Passwords and one-time codes are never logged.
  • Why (lawful bases under GDPR). To provide the service you request (contract/consent), to keep it secure (legitimate interests), and to comply with law.
  • Messages, forums and notifications. Direct messages between classmates are stored so the conversation can be shown to both people. They are not visible to other learners. Teachers of the course can read them only if the course has that setting switched on, and every conversation then says so. If a message is reported, the course's teachers and our administrators can read that conversation to deal with the report. If you block someone, they can't message you in any course and you no longer see their forum posts. Course forum posts are visible to people viewing that course. In-app notifications are kept for up to 180 days (read ones 90 days). Notification emails go only to you, carry no message text, and you can choose which you get on the Notifications page. Journal entries are private to you unless you choose to share one with your teacher. Anonymous survey answers are stored without your account.
  • Cookies. We use only essential cookies/local storage needed for sign-in and to remember your progress. If you use two-factor authentication and tick "Remember this device for 30 days", we set one more essential cookie on that device so it can skip the code for 30 days; we store only a one-way hash of it, and you can revoke it by turning two-factor off, changing your password, or using "Sign out everywhere". No advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.
  • Your rights (GDPR). Access, rectification, erasure ("right to be forgotten"), restriction, portability, and objection. You can export or delete your data from your account or by contacting us.
  • Your rights (CCPA/CPRA). Right to know, delete, correct, and opt out of "sale"/"sharing" — we do not sell or share personal information. We will not discriminate for exercising these rights.
  • Retention. We keep account data while your account is active and delete it on request or after prolonged inactivity. Security and activity logs follow the periods above (network details 90 days; log entries up to 2 years). When you delete your account, log entries keep only an opaque account identifier so the security record stays intact. Deleting your account also deletes your direct-message conversations (both sides), journal entries, notifications and named survey answers.
  • Security. Encryption in transit (TLS) and at rest, least-privilege access, and the practices in our security model.
  • Children. Not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country); we do not knowingly collect their data.
  • International transfers & contact. Where data crosses borders we use appropriate safeguards. Privacy requests: Contact.

Content, sources & licensing

The Platform is only as legitimate as the rights behind its content. We draw on three kinds of material, and nothing else:

  • Material we own. Content from the Sikh Archive and its associated digitised library (our HuggingFace dataset) which we control and have the right to use. We still audit our own collections for any third-party items before redistributing them.
  • Openly-licensed content. Works released under open licenses (for example Creative Commons), used strictly under the terms of their license. We preserve attribution and license notices, honour share-alike conditions where they apply, and respect non-commercial restrictions — the Platform is free, and any non-commercial material would be excluded from any paid offering.
  • Third-party content used with written permission. Where material belongs to someone else and is not openly licensed, we use it only with that owner's written permission. We do not scrape or rehost copyrighted third-party videos, books, or courses; where appropriate we embed a creator's own public video or link out to the original instead.

Gurbani is public-domain scripture, presented faithfully and sourced with care. Modern translations and transliterations may be copyrighted, so we use only public-domain or openly-licensed renderings, or our own.

Original Sikhi University course material may not be re-sold. Third-party and openly-licensed materials remain under their own licenses, with attribution and notices preserved. Trademarks of other institutions do not imply their endorsement of the Platform.
